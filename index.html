About the company

Who
we are.

FTS Elevators & Escalators (Fixable Technical Services LLC) is a Dubai-based vertical transportation company delivering complete elevator and escalator solutions across the UAE. From elegant villa lifts to high-traffic commercial installations, we combine certified engineering, genuine components and disciplined after-sales care so your building never stops moving.
